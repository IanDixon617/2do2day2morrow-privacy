Privacy Policy for 2do2day2morrow

Last updated: 9 October 2026

2do2day2morrow ("the app") is designed with your privacy as a core principle.

We do not collect any data.

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or content you enter. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login required to use the app.

Everything stays on your device.

All tasks, settings, and any photos you choose to add, whether attached to a task or set as a background via "My theme", are stored locally on your own iPhone, using standard iOS storage. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

Exporting and sharing.

If you use "Export my tasks" or the Share option on a task, the app creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it. We never receive it.

No analytics or tracking.

The app does not use analytics, advertising identifiers, or any third-party tracking services.

In-app purchases.

If you choose to unlock the paid features of the app, that purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, see, or store any payment or card information — this is handled solely by Apple under its own privacy practices.

Notifications.

The app can send you local reminders for tasks you've set. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device and are never sent to or processed by any external server.

Backups.

If you back up your iPhone (via iCloud or your computer), your app data may be included in that backup, subject to your own backup settings and Apple's privacy policies for iCloud — this is not something we control or have access to.

Children's privacy.

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information from any user.

Changes to this policy.

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds features that do collect data — this page will be updated, and the "last updated" date above will reflect that.

Contact.

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at iandixon_uk@yahoo.co.uk.
